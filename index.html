How it works

From application to reimbursement.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Submit competition, project and requested funding details through the student portal.

  2. 02

    Review

    A faculty endorser and the School review eligibility and the funding request.

  3. 03

    Subsidy

    Receive the funding decision and submit eligible receipts for reimbursement.

  4. 04

    Report results

    Report the official outcome within one month of its announcement.

Eligibility

Check before you begin.

Every application should meet these core requirements.

  • Yes

    Current student

    You are a current full-time PolyU School of Design student.

  • Yes

    Recognised opportunity

    The competition or award has professional, national or international recognition.

  • Yes

    Original funding request

    The entry is not supported by another financial scheme.

  • No

    Exchange student

    Exchange students are not eligible for this scheme.
